# AirTouch Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

AirTouch uses the device's front camera only after you start a gesture session and grant camera permission. If you leave AirTouch or turn off the screen during a session, Android keeps the camera foreground service active and displays the system camera indicator and an ongoing session notification. Stop the session in AirTouch or use the notification's Stop camera action. Force-stopping the app ends the session.

Camera frames are processed on your device for hand-gesture recognition. AirTouch does not save, upload, or share camera frames. The app does not create an account or collect analytics in this version.

If you enable palm-to-lock, Android Device Administrator access is used only to request the screen lock after the configured open-palm gesture. You can disable that access in Android system settings.

If you enable gesture scrolling, Android's Accessibility service checks the active window for scrollable controls and sends a scroll action after a two-finger up/down swipe during an active camera session. AirTouch does not collect or store screen text. You can disable the service at any time in Android Accessibility settings.

AirTouch does not sell personal information. This policy describes the current app build; if the app's data handling changes, this policy must be updated before that version is published.

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support contact shown on the app's Google Play listing.
